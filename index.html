About Vigorish Media

Building Brands
That Add Value.

Vigorish Media is a creative media and digital marketing company headquartered at Chibuluma Road, New Kasama, Lusaka, Zambia. We exist to help businesses build stronger brands through design, branding, social media, digital marketing, photography, videography and web solutions.

Our Origin

Founded with an unwavering conviction that Zambian and African enterprises deserve world-standard creative execution, Vigorish Media blends aesthetic mastery with analytical business rigour. We don't just create visuals — we engineer strategic leverage.

The Name "Vigorish"

In finance and strategy, "vigorish" represents the built-in structural advantage that tilts outcomes toward success. For our clients, that advantage is uncompromising brand distinction, high perceived value, and measurable market authority.

Our Vision

To be Zambia’s most trusted creative catalyst, redefining African corporate storytelling with world-class execution.

Our Philosophy

Design must not merely look beautiful; it must solve business bottlenecks, build trust, and add tangible enterprise value.

Precision Craft

From 300DPI print calibration to sub-second mobile page loads, every single detail is tuned to perfection.
